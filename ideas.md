# Govan Electrical Website Design Philosophy

## Selected Design Approach: Professional Industrial Elegance

**Design Movement:** Modern Industrial with Corporate Professionalism
**Core Principles:**
1. **Bold Yellow & Black Contrast** - High-visibility branding that commands attention and conveys energy/expertise
2. **Structured Hierarchy** - Clear information architecture with prominent CTAs for service inquiries
3. **Trust Through Professionalism** - Clean typography, organized layouts, and quality imagery that builds confidence
4. **Functional Minimalism** - No unnecessary decoration; every element serves a purpose in guiding the customer journey

**Color Philosophy:**
- **Primary: Vibrant Yellow (#FFD700)** - Represents energy, reliability, and electrical expertise. Used for accents, highlights, and CTAs
- **Secondary: Deep Black (#1A1A1A)** - Conveys professionalism, stability, and trustworthiness. Used for text and structural elements
- **Tertiary: Light Gray (#F5F5F5)** - Provides breathing room and contrast against the bold primary colors
- **Accent: Dark Charcoal (#333333)** - Used for secondary text and subtle UI elements

**Layout Paradigm:**
- **Asymmetric Hero Section** - Yellow accent bar on left with company logo and tagline, contact info on right side
- **Service Grid** - Three-column layout showcasing Solar, Electrical, and Diesel services
- **Testimonial Carousel** - Client reviews displayed in a rotating carousel for social proof
- **Contact Form Integration** - Prominent inquiry form positioned in the middle of the page for easy access

**Signature Elements:**
1. **Yellow Vertical Bar** - Left-side accent bar that frames the header and creates visual interest
2. **Bold Typography** - Large, confident headlines in black with yellow underlines or accents
3. **Service Icons** - Distinctive icons for each service category (solar panels, electrical outlet, diesel tank)

**Interaction Philosophy:**
- **Hover Effects** - Subtle color transitions and slight scale increases on interactive elements
- **Smooth Scrolling** - Page sections flow naturally with gentle fade-in animations as they come into view
- **CTA Prominence** - "Get a Quote" and "View Services" buttons are always visible and inviting

**Animation Guidelines:**
- **Entrance Animations** - Elements fade in and slide up slightly as they enter the viewport (200-300ms duration)
- **Hover States** - Buttons and cards scale slightly (1.02x) with a 150ms ease-out transition
- **Carousel Transitions** - Testimonials fade and slide between items with a 400ms duration
- **Scroll-Triggered Reveals** - Service cards and stats animate in sequence as user scrolls

**Typography System:**
- **Display Font: Montserrat Bold** - For main headlines and company name (creates strong presence)
- **Body Font: Open Sans Regular** - For body text and descriptions (highly readable)
- **Accent Font: Montserrat SemiBold** - For subheadings and section titles
- **Hierarchy:** H1 (48px), H2 (36px), H3 (24px), Body (16px), Small (14px)
